HRLX Photo Care Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Photos and videos

Photo Care requests Photos access so it can show and review the media you authorize, compare similar items, measure local storage opportunities, create a smaller video copy only when you explicitly request it, and request user-confirmed changes such as deletion. Limited Photos Access is supported.

On-device analysis

Photo, screenshot, OCR, similarity, and quality analysis is designed to run on the device. The current app does not upload your photos, videos, OCR text, or Photo Library identifiers to an HRLX-operated server for analysis.

Local app data

The app may store preferences and derived analysis information such as review decisions, indexes, and cache entries in its local container. There is no HRLX user account or HRLX cloud copy of your Photo Library.

Deletion

Photo Care does not automatically delete media. A deletion is requested only after you explicitly continue, and iOS Photos provides the final system confirmation. Apple Photos manages Recently Deleted behavior.

Purchases

Photo Care Pro Lifetime is offered through Apple In-App Purchase. Apple StoreKit and your Apple Account handle purchase and entitlement information. Photo Care does not operate a separate payment account system.

Analytics, advertising, tracking, and sharing

The current app contains no advertising SDK, analytics SDK, app-owned tracking system, or tracking domains, and does not share your Photo Library media or on-device analysis with advertising or analytics partners.

Your controls

You can use Limited Photos Access, change or revoke Photos permission in iOS Settings, choose every Keep/Delete action, restore Pro Lifetime through StoreKit, and remove local app data by deleting the app. Removing the app does not itself delete media from your Photo Library.

Contact

Privacy and support questions: hrlxapp@icloud.com.

ملخص الخصوصية بالعربية

صُمم Photo Care لمعالجة الصور والتحليل محليًا على الجهاز. لا يرفع الإصدار الحالي الصور أو الفيديوهات أو نصوص OCR إلى خادم HRLX للتحليل، ولا يتضمن إعلانات أو أدوات تتبع أو تحليلات خارجية. أنت تتحكم في صلاحية الصور وفي كل قرار حذف، ويظل تأكيد iOS النهائي مطلوبًا للحذف.

لأسئلة الخصوصية: hrlxapp@icloud.com.
